Usage rules

Do

  • Navy for BASE
  • Grey for Kiosk / App / Consulting
  • Amber for OS badge and arc accents only
  • Black masters for print
  • Green / red only in status UIs

Don’t

  • No teal or rainbow accents
  • No gradients on logos
  • No multi-color silhouettes
  • Don’t swap amber into body text
  • Don’t restyle BASE in grey
